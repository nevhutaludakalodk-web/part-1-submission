Nutritious Community Meals

Every week, our shelter kitchen in Polokwane serves warm, balanced, and fresh meals to over 500 individuals in need. Take a look at our daily menu programs.

Sponsor a Meal ($2) Volunteer in Kitchen

Weekly Meal Schedule

Our meals are carefully prepared by kitchen volunteers under the guidance of local nutritionists to ensure well-rounded, healthy, and delicious food.

Morning Breakfast Scheme

Serving Time: 07:00 AM – 08:30 AM Daily

Enriched maize porridge (Pap), boiled eggs, whole grain toast with fruit spread, tea, and warm milk for children.

Hearty Hot Lunch

Serving Time: 12:30 PM – 02:00 PM Daily

Traditional South African beef or chicken stew with steamed vegetables, samp & beans, or savory mealie pap.

Evening Soup & Bread

Serving Time: 05:00 PM – 06:30 PM Daily

Nourishing vegetable lentil soup, split pea soup, freshly baked bread loaves, and hot cocoa for shelter residents.

Special Dietary Programs

We ensure that vulnerable children, elderly citizens, and individuals with health requirements receive specialized food care.

Youth & School Lunchpacks

Packed lunches containing peanut butter sandwiches, seasonal apples, bananas, and juice boxes distributed to local rural schools.

Community Garden Harvest

Fresh spinach, tomatoes, carrots, and cabbage grown right in our Polokwane community garden to supplement all cooked meals.

Take-Home Food Parcels

Monthly hampers packed with rice, maize meal, cooking oil, canned beans, and milk powder for registered low-income households.
