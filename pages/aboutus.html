About Our Foundation

Discover our history, mission, and the team driving social change in Limpopo.

Our Organizational History

Founded in 2018 in Polokwane, Hope for All Foundation started as a small group of volunteers feeding homeless individuals on weekends. Over the past six years, it has grown into a registered community organisation providing shelter, hot meals, and skill development workshops.

What began as a weekend initiative in a garage serving 20 individuals has transformed into a community refuge impacting thousands of lives every month.

Our Mission

To empower vulnerable individuals in local communities by providing basic necessities, education, and career development tools.

Our Vision

A compassionate society where every person has access to food, shelter, and opportunities to thrive.
