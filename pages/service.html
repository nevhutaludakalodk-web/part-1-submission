Our Programs & Community Services

Providing direct support through targeted social upliftment initiatives.

1. Emergency Feeding Scheme

Our mobile soup kitchen operates five days a week across underprivileged areas in Polokwane. We ensure that families, youth, and elderly community members receive balanced nutrition prepared under clean conditions.

  • Daily hot soup and bread distribution
  • Monthly family grocery relief parcels
  • Emergency nutrition packages for child-headed households

2. Transitional Shelter Care

We operate a 50-bed transitional shelter facility providing short-term housing for individuals transitioning out of homelessness, emergency crises, or family displace situations.

  • Secure overnight accommodation with clean bedding
  • Ablution facilities and personal hygiene packs
  • Social worker consultations and counseling

3. Vocational Skills Development

Our practical training program bridges the gap to sustainable employment by teaching basic computing, literacy, professional trades, and entrepreneurship.

  • Basic Computer Literacy and Internet Skills
  • CV Writing & Job Interview Coaching
  • Crafts, Sewing, and Small Business Training
