Our Core Objectives

Founded in 2018 in Polokwane, our mission is to eliminate local hunger and build sustainable paths out of poverty.

Nutritious Feeding Schemes

Serving over 500 balanced hot meals weekly to homeless individuals and low-income families across Polokwane.

Temporary Safe Shelter

Providing secure, warm, and hygienic temporary housing alongside healthcare access for vulnerable residents.

Skills Workshops

Offering vocational computer literacy, resume building, and trade skills to equip adults for long-term employment.
